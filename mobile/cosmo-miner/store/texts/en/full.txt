Mine Cosmo — a relaxing cosmic idle adventure.

Build your mining empire across distant planets, upgrade ships and crew, fight off alien raiders, and rise from a lone prospector to galactic tycoon.

Core gameplay:
- Tap to mine — extract iron, titan, iridium, void crystals, and more
- Upgrade your fleet — unlock new ships with unique perks
- Recruit characters — captains, engineers, scientists, each with bonuses
- Explore planets — every world has its own resource mix and aliens
- Battle alien raiders — defend your fleet in tactical encounters
- Expedition mode — send ships on multi-stage missions
- Prestige — restart with permanent boosts when progress slows
- Achievements — dozens of milestones to unlock

Designed for short sessions, but rewarding deep play. No forced timers, no pay-to-win.
